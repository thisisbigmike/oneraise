The Problem

Half the world is locked out of global crowdfunding.

The access gap

  • GoFundMe, Kickstarter, Stripe — block most of Africa, LatAm, South Asia, Middle East
  • Creators with real causes can't even open an account

The trust gap

  • Funds that do get through move on slow, costly rails
  • Weeks of delay, lost to fees and FX
  • Donors can't verify their money achieved anything

Billions in goodwill capital never reaches the people who need it — or arrives with no proof it worked.

Why Now

The rails finally exist.

$160B+

<$0.01
Solana transaction cost — micro-donations finally viable
Africa
Fastest-growing stablecoin adoption region; mobile-money native
  • Tether and stablecoin issuers expanding aggressively into African corridors
  • Local rails (Busha, Raenest, Paystack) now bridge crypto ↔ local currency instantly
  • Africans are adopting stablecoins faster than anywhere on earth — to escape inflation and broken cross-border banking. The demand is already here; the trust layer isn't.
The Solution

Money held in escrow.
Released only on verified milestones.

  • Contributions held in stablecoin escrow on Solana — settles in seconds, near-zero cost, no FX loss
  • Funds release to creators only as predefined milestones are verified — the "Protect" protocol
  • Connects African rails (Busha, Raenest, Paystack) to global liquidity — anyone, anywhere can fund anyone, anywhere
  • On-chain proof of impact for every release; Cloak adds donor privacy with an auditable compliance trail

We don't just move money. We move trust.

Why Crypto / Why Solana

Remove the chain and the product breaks.

What only crypto enables

  • Permissionless access — no bank account, no platform gatekeeping
  • Trustless escrow — funds locked by code, not a company holding your money
  • Verifiable proof — every disbursement on-chain, publicly auditable

Why Solana specifically

  • Sub-cent fees make small cross-border donations viable
  • Seconds to settle — creators get funds fast
  • Mature stablecoin + USDC liquidity
