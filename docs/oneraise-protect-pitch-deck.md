# OneRaise Protect Pitch Deck

## Core Positioning

**OneRaise Protect is the escrow and verification layer for high-trust crowdfunding, emergency aid, and grant distribution.**

The broader OneRaise platform helps people raise globally. OneRaise Protect answers the harder question:

**How do backers know funds will only move when progress is proven?**

Use this deck when pitching:
- emergency aid campaigns
- medical fundraising
- community infrastructure projects
- NGO fund distribution
- DAO and hackathon grant payouts
- large campaigns where backers need extra assurance

## Slide 3: The Solution

Title:
**Protect turns donation flow into a verified funding lifecycle.**

Lifecycle:
- **Deposit:** backers or sponsors fund escrow in supported payment rails or stablecoins.
- **Lock:** funds are reserved against campaign rules, milestone thresholds, or verification requirements.
- **Verify:** creators submit documents, receipts, photos, reports, proof-of-work, or vendor details.
- **Release:** approved funds unlock to creators, vendors, or grantees.
- **Refund:** failed or unverified campaigns keep funds refundable according to campaign rules.

Suggested visual:
Horizontal flow:
Deposit -> Lock -> Verify -> Release or Refund.

Speaker note:
Make this slide operational. Protect is valuable because every state of the money is explicit.

## 60-Second Pitch Script

OneRaise Protect is the escrow and verification layer for high-trust crowdfunding.

Normal crowdfunding is built to collect money. But for emergency aid, community projects, and grants, collection is not enough. Backers need to know funds will only move when progress is proven, and recipients need payment rails that work across borders.

Protect solves this by turning fundraising into a verified lifecycle. Backers deposit funds into a protected campaign. Funds can stay locked until milestones, documents, receipts, proof-of-work, or verifier approvals are submitted. Approved milestones unlock payouts to creators, vendors, or grantees. If a campaign fails its conditions, funds can remain refundable or be redirected according to campaign rules.

This makes OneRaise useful for protected crowdfunding, medical aid escrow, and grant distribution. Our wedge is simple: serious campaigns need more than a donation page. They need trust infrastructure.

OneRaise Protect helps money move with proof.

## Slide Design Rules

- Use product UI more than photography.
- Make escrow status visible on almost every slide.
- Use status language consistently: Funding, Locked, Pending Verification, Released, Refunded.
- Show balances as separated buckets: Total Protected, Refundable, Pending Review, Released.
- Use green for approved and released states.
- Use gold for pending verification.
- Use blue for refundable funds.
- Do not overuse crypto imagery. Protect is about accountability, not speculation.
